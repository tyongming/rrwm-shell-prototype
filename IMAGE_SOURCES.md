## Production rule

Prefer, in order:

1. Client-owned product, warehouse, and team photography with written approval.
2. Paid stock with a stored commercial license and any required releases.
3. Purpose-generated brand imagery reviewed for unwanted logos or protected content.

Do not publish an image when its source page, license, or third-party rights
cannot be documented.
